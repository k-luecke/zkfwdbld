=== GENERALITY PROBE (does this unlock the signature-scope class, or only this bug?) ===
The detect->emit frame is GENERAL over schemes that sign a digest of a bytes preimage
(EIP-191 personal_sign / raw ecrecover): only the digest expression changes, and the
signer takes (pk, preimage). It does NOT yet cover EIP-712 typed data: that digest is
built from a domain separator + typehash + per-field struct encoding, not an opaque
preimage. EIP-712 is DETECTED (and the emitter refuses rather than guesses) but NOT
emitted. So this keystone unlocks the EIP-191/raw signature class; the permit /
signature-scope class (the deferred Centrifuge frontier) needs a typed-data digest
builder, not just this signer. Transfers in SHAPE, not yet in COVERAGE.
