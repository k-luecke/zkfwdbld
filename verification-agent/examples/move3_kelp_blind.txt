=== TWO HONEST WALLS (the work order) ===
1. SCREENING WAS TOO LOOSE. "access-control protocol" is not enough; an LST's real
   bugs are typically accounting/oracle, a different class. Screen specifically for
   the alt-entrypoint SHAPE: a judged finding where a permissionless path reaches an
   effect a sibling GATES. Kelp does not have it (Seer 0 paths corroborates).
2. SCORING IS OFFLINE-BLOCKED. Kelp's judged H/M findings live on the Code4rena site,
   not in the repo; offline they can't be fetched, and scoring on fuzzy model recall
   would violate the blind discipline. So whether any lead maps to a real finding is
   UNSCORED. (Moot for a catch here, since Seer pathed nothing.)

=== WHAT THIS SESSION PROVED ===
The full finding loop runs BLIND, end-to-end, on a fresh contest (enabled by a
general M0 build-info fix so Slither consumes Foundry build-info offline). And under
blind conditions on a wrong-shape contest, the sharpened hunter + Seer + gate
discipline produced NO false catch. The first blind CATCH now needs a contest screened
for the alt-entrypoint shape + a way to fetch the answer key to score. Both are
mechanical next steps, not capability gaps.
